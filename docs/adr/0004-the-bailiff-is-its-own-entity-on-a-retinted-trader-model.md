# The Bailiff is an entity of its own, drawn on a retinted wandering trader

The Bailiff is the Pillar's own entity type, with its own goals (seek remembered containers, steal, sleep at night, flee), but for now it is drawn with vanilla's wandering-trader model and vanilla's own trader texture, tinted crimson as it is drawn. A retinted copy of the texture would be Mojang's art in the Pillar's jar under the Pillar's licence; a tint ships none of it. A bespoke model and texture can replace the look later without touching behaviour.

**Considered.** The jam draft's buffed vindicator with a name: it inherits every change a pack or vanilla makes to vindicators, and its goals had to be stripped by reflection. A bespoke model now: art the first release doesn't need to prove the loop. A retinted copy of the trader's texture shipped in the jar: the look the tint gives, but redistributing Mojang's art.
