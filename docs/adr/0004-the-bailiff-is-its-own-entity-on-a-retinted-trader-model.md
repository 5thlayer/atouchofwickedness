# The Bailiff is an entity of its own, drawn on a retinted wandering trader

The Bailiff is the Pillar's own entity type, with its own goals (seek remembered containers, steal, sleep at night, flee), but for now it is drawn with vanilla's wandering-trader model and a retinted texture of the Pillar's own. A bespoke model can replace the look later without touching behaviour.

**Considered.** The jam draft's buffed vindicator with a name: it inherits every change a pack or vanilla makes to vindicators, and its goals had to be stripped by reflection. A bespoke model now: art the first release doesn't need to prove the loop.
