# Changelog

Written for Consumers: what a mod building against A Touch of Wickedness can use, or will see change. A published version never changes: a fix is the next patch (`docs/agents/releases.md`).

## Unreleased

- A Bailiff picking up an item from the ground, its player's drops or anyone's, now discovers `seizure` for its player, as taking a stack from them or their containers does.

## 0.1.1

- Discovery: a player learns each of the Pillar's things by meeting it, as in A Touch of Darkness and A Touch of Madness. Each is a hidden advancement in the Pillar's own tab, `atouchofwickedness:discovery/<id>`, with a toast: `wicked_kill`, `gossip`, `marked`, `overdue`, `forfeit`, `bailiff`, `seizure`, `owing`, `atonement`, `seal`, `tally_stick`, `strongbox` and `bailiffs_bag`. A pack that removes one finds it known, and `discovery` in the common config, on by default, switches them all off, every thing then known. The new criterion triggers are `atouchofwickedness:wicked_kill`, `standing` (with a `standing` of `marked`, `overdue` or `forfeit`, met by that one or any above it), `bailiff_came`, `seized`, `owing`, `atoned` and `gossiped` (#19).
- With EMI installed, information pages on Wickedness, the Standings, Atonement, the Bailiff, the Seal, the Tally Stick, the Strongbox and the Bailiff's Bag, each reading "Not yet discovered" until its Discovery. None gives the Threshold, a band, a weight, the Decay or an Atonement's size (#19).
- The Pillar nests A Touch Of's Runtime, which keeps the Discovery record. Wickedness is still never drawn (#19).

## 0.1.0

- Killing a creature adds its weight to the killer's hidden Wickedness: 2 for livestock, 5 for wild animals, 15 for rare and companion creatures, 30 for a villager and 50 for a cat or ocelot. The weights are the data map `atouchofwickedness:wickedness_weight` on entity types, unlisted meaning 0, so a datapack can weigh any creature.
- A kill is credited as vanilla credits its experience: to the player who last hurt the creature, by arrow, trident or a trap they knocked it into too, or to the owner of the tamed pet that killed it. A kill no player had a hand in counts for no one, and Creative and Spectator players gain nothing and are never visited.
- Any tamed creature weighs 50 whoever owns it, a baby double, and a neutral mob that went for its killer before they struck it nothing (an iron golem always counts). Each damaging hit short of the killing blow on a creature weighing 30 or more adds 3. The tamed weight, the baby multiplier, the hit cost and the weight it starts from are in the common config.
- Dying leaves Wickedness and the Threshold unchanged.
- Wickedness falls by 10 each in-game day while the player is online, a point at a time, a night slept through included, and never below 0. The daily Decay is in the common config.
- Kind acts take Wickedness off at once, credited to whoever did them: breeding two animals 3, taming one 10, feeding a hurt tamed pet the player owns 1 per heart it regains, curing a zombie villager 40, and a villager picking up food the player threw 2, at most 5 times a day. Creative and Spectator players atone for nothing. Each amount and the daily gift cap are in the common config.
- Each player draws a hidden Threshold from 50 to 100. When their Wickedness reaches it, a Bailiff arrives 12–24 blocks away, announced by a high-pitched Wither blast at the player, subtitled "Bailiff arrives", Wickedness resets to 0 and a new Threshold is drawn.
- After a Bailiff arrives, no other comes for the same player for 5 minutes, `secondsBetweenVisits` in the common config. A Bailiff looks for its spot in every direction around the player, and while the player is still at or above their Threshold, as when the last found no spot or the cooldown ran, the visit stays owed and one tries to arrive every 3 seconds, `secondsBetweenRetries` in the common config, Wickedness resetting only once one arrives; a dead player's waits until they respawn (#9).
- With nothing of its player's to rob, the Bailiff goes for them, and its first hit that lands takes one stack from the main inventory or hotbar, never the held item, armour or the off hand. Then it taunts them and flees, as after robbing the base. A player with nothing worth taking it taunts at once, with "Nothing? The Bailiff will return.", and a Bailiff that vanishes with nothing puts the player's Wickedness back up to their Threshold, so another comes once the cooldown is over. After 60 s on site it flees whatever it has, faster than a sprinting player; 24 blocks from its player, or after 20 s of fleeing, it stands still for 2 s charging a teleport, when it can still be killed from afar, then vanishes in a woosh with what it took. Killed, it drops what it stole and 1–3 Seals. The vanishing distance and the charging time are in the common config.
- Until it has its haul, a Bailiff walks to an item lying within 6 blocks of it, `pickupRadius` in the common config, and picks it up as its haul, its dead player's drops included, then taunts them once they are back; its player dying no longer ends the visit. On Peaceful it still comes and still steals, its hits dealing no damage, though a shield its player raises its way still stops it, and it is never despawned. Each wicked player gets a Bailiff of their own, which goes only for them, and anyone may fight and kill it. It is gone once its player logs out, the server stopping included, or changes dimension, and none is left after a restart; one that goes with nothing leaves its player owing, as one that vanishes with nothing does, so leaving buys no escape (#9).
- The Bailiff weighs what it takes by Value, the data map `atouchofwickedness:bailiff_value` on items, which a datapack can extend: from a container or its player it draws one stack, each as likely as its share of what all those it may take are worth, a stack being worth its items' Value times its count. An item a pack hasn't valued is worth 1, `unlistedValue` in the common config, one valued 0 is never taken, and each enchantment level adds 20, a book's stored ones included, `valuePerEnchantmentLevel`. On the ground it goes only for an item worth at least 5 each, `pickupFloor`, so junk dropped in front of it no longer buys off a visit. By default dirt, sand, gravel, cobblestone and stone, netherrack, rotten flesh and seeds are worth nothing, food and lapis 5, iron 10, gold 20, emeralds 40, diamonds and shulker boxes 100, diamond gear 300 a piece, netherite ingots 600 and netherite gear 900 (#18).
- A Bailiff that arrives at night lies down where it arrived and sleeps until dawn, Zs rising over it, robbing no one. The first hit on a sleeping Bailiff deals double damage and wakes it, without sending it fleeing, and its 60 s on site start only once it wakes (#8).
- When its player opened Seizable containers within 32 blocks of where it arrived, the Bailiff robs the base instead of mugging them: it walks to the nearest, opens it, lid and sound, and takes one stack, its whole haul, moving on to the next when it holds nothing worth taking. Then it walks up to its player, shakes its head at them with a mocking sound and "The Bailiff takes its due." in the action bar, and flees. Hit by its player as it robs or taunts, it flees at once with whatever it has. Only the last 32 containers a player opened are remembered, by dimension and position, so another player's chest is never robbed. Seizable is the block entity type tag `atouchofwickedness:seizable`, chests, trapped chests and barrels by default, which a datapack can extend; ender chests, shulker boxes and Strongboxes never are, even when a datapack adds them. The number remembered and the robbing radius are in the common config.
- Warnings, heard by the player alone and each with a subtitle: a far bell 24 blocks off as they enter Marked (½ of their Threshold) and every 2 minutes while there, chains 12 blocks off at Overdue (¾) every minute, and footsteps 4 blocks off at Forfeit (90%) every 30 s. While a player is Forfeit, passive animals within 16 blocks that see them flee from them, on any difficulty, a tamed one never. The intervals, the distances, the flee radius and the animals' flee speeds are in the common config.
- Four Seals around a chest craft a Strongbox, a chest of the Pillar's own in dark stone with a crimson Seal for its lock, its art built from Malcolm Riley's unused textures (CC BY 4.0), which holds 27 stacks, never joins another into a double chest, and is never robbed by a Bailiff.
- A Seal and a stick craft a Tally Stick, which shows the player's Standing (Clean, Marked, Overdue or Forfeit) in the action bar, wears 1 of 16 uses each time, and repairs with a Seal in an anvil.
- `/wickedness get|set|add <player> [value]` and `/wickedness threshold <player>`, at permission level 2.
- Every number of the loop is in the common config, `atouchofwickedness-common.toml`.
- With Jade installed, looking at a Bailiff shows the player it came for and the stacks it has taken, and nothing of anyone's Wickedness. Jade is optional, and the Pillar plays without it (#13).
- A killed Bailiff now and then drops a Bailiff's Bag, a bundle that holds four times what a bundle holds, filled and emptied as one is, with a bar and a tooltip that read its fill against its own size. The chance is 5%, `bagChance` in the common config, and each level of Looting on its killer's weapon adds 1%, `bagChancePerLootingLevel`. The Bag is in the creative tab, and it isn't a bundle to dye (#10).
- Each villager within 16 blocks who can see a Wicked kill gains vanilla's minor negative gossip about the killer, 5 times the kill's weight, so a cow adds 10 and a villager 150: their prices rise, and once the gossip is bad enough iron golems turn on the killer by vanilla's own rules. The radius and the scale are `gossip.radius` and `gossip.scale` in the common config (#11).
- With A Touch of Madness installed, a Bailiff nearby drains Sanity like any hostile mob: the Pillar adds it to `#atouchofmadness:unsettling` as an optional entry, and without Madness the tag is never read (#12).

- The Pillar has its own creative tab, "A Touch of Wickedness", and no longer nests `libworks-runtime` or fills its Works tab.
